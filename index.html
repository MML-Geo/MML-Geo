About the Project

MML-GEO aims to develop innovative multiscale approaches for understanding, modelling and predicting the behaviour of geomaterials through the integration of advanced numerical modelling, experimental observations and machine learning techniques.

Research Programme

WP1

Experimental Characterization

Laboratory and field observations across multiple scales.

WP2

Multiscale Modelling

Development of advanced numerical models for geomaterials.

WP3

Machine Learning

Data-driven and hybrid physics-informed approaches.

WP4

Applications

Underground structures, geotechnics and energy systems.
